# Occasion collections, draft for the owner's red pen

Drafted 2026-08-15. These are the human-authored recipes for the reactive
collections program: each is a name, a data rule, and a season. The system
fills, gates, ranks, and schedules them nightly; nobody curates a title by
hand. Names are the part that matters most and the part most worth editing.
Cut freely, rename freely. A recipe ships only after it survives this file.

Every fill count below was measured against the live catalog on 2026-08-15,
restricted to indexable titles (the corroboration gate). The minting rule:
a recipe must fill with at least 20 qualifying titles or it does not become
a page, same as every other collection kind.

Two ideas were cut for failing honesty, listed at the bottom.

## Cut for failing honesty

- **A good cry.** No stored fact distinguishes a tearjerker from a drama.
  Inventing the distinction would be a machine guessing at feelings, which is
  the slop this whole system exists to refuse. Revisit if a real signal ever
  exists.
- **Sweater weather / cozy fall.** Same problem. "Cozy" is not in the data.

## Mechanics (applies to every recipe above)

- A recipe mints a page only when it fills with 20+ qualifying titles from
  the indexable pool. Below that, no page, no exceptions.
- Selection uses the confidence-adjusted score, display order uses the
  Balasaur Score, identical to every existing collection.
- Seasonal windows control prominence on the homepage rail and hub, never
  existence. Pages persist year-round and keep their search equity; the
  Halloween page in March is simply not promoted.
- Slugs are stable and human ("/best/date-night-movies"). Renaming a
  collection's display name never changes its slug.
- Approximate fills marked "~" or "needs count check" get measured exactly
  before their recipe ships. A recipe that measures under 20 is cut or
  loosened here first.

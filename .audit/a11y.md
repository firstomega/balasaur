SCORE_AGREE: yes

SUMMARY:
The accessibility fundamentals here are deliberate, not accidental, and that is unusual for a solo part-time build. Every one of the 16 `<img>` tags in src/ has an explicit alt, and the split is correct: real text where the image carries meaning (MediaCard.tsx:242 `alt={item.title}`) and `alt=""` where it does not (the backdrop at MediaDetail.tsx:299, provider logos, search thumbnails). A single global focus ring is defined at styles.css:170, and tabbing 22 stops through the home page at both 390px and 1440px produced a visible 2px blue outline on every stop, measuring 5.10:1 against the page ground where 3:1 is the requirement. The token comments in styles.css show the contrast was actually computed before shipping, and re-deriving all 27 pairs myself confirms the numbers: body text 13.12:1, secondary text 7.30:1, the dimmest text 5.28:1. The main defect is that the skip link is dead on the pages that matter most. It points at `#main`, and `<main id="main">` is absent from movie.$id.tsx, tv.$id.tsx, person.$id.tsx, night.index.tsx, night.$code.tsx, watched.tsx and triage.tsx, so on roughly 62,000 title pages a keyboard user presses the skip link and nothing happens. I verified this live: on /about the target exists, on /night it does not.

For the business goals in front of you, this category is not where the money is. AdSense review does not test accessibility, and nothing here is blocking indexation. Heading structure and alt text are the parts that also feed search, and both are in decent shape.

STRENGTHS:
- Images are handled correctly across the board. 16 `<img>` tags, all with an explicit alt, and the decorative-versus-meaningful call is right in every case I checked (MediaDetail.tsx:299 backdrop empty, MediaDetail.tsx:322 poster named, CollectionRail.tsx:27 empty, play.tsx:344 descriptive).
- Focus is visible everywhere. The rule at styles.css:170 sets a 2px #3b82f6 outline with 2px offset; I tabbed 22 stops on the home page at 390px and 1440px and 9 stops on /night, and every stop rendered it. Measured 5.10:1 against #101216 and 4.70:1 against the panel colour, both well clear of the 3:1 floor for a non-text indicator.
- The colour tokens were computed, not eyeballed. Body text 13.12:1, muted text 7.30:1 on the ground and 6.73:1 on panels, the dimmest text 5.28:1, and the blue button's dark label 5.26:1. The comment at styles.css:88 records that white on the blue measured 3.68:1 and was rejected, which is exactly right.
- Radix overlays are wired properly with no exceptions. Every Dialog and Sheet has a title, including the two visually hidden ones at MediaDetail.tsx:731 and :751, and TopBar.tsx:47 deliberately clears `aria-describedby` with a comment saying why. The search box at TopBarSearch.tsx:149 is a full combobox: role, aria-expanded, aria-controls, aria-autocomplete, and role="option" with aria-selected on every result.
- Zero clickable `<div>`s in src/components/balasaur and src/routes. Everything interactive is a real button or link, which is why the tab order came out clean. `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured globally at styles.css:150 and again per-component on the card hover zoom.

GAPS:
- The skip link is a no-op on the entire title-page surface. `<main id="main">` is missing from movie.$id.tsx, tv.$id.tsx, person.$id.tsx, watched.tsx, triage.tsx and both Movie Night routes. Rendered /night and confirmed: the link resolves to `#main`, and `document.querySelector("#main")` returns nothing. Those pages also have no `<main>` landmark at all, so a screen reader has no "jump to content" route on the pages search traffic would land on. MediaDetail returns an `<article>` where the `<main>` should wrap it.
- The "Hide seen" toggle has no accessible name. index.tsx:805 wraps a Radix `<Switch>` and a text span in a `<label>`, but Radix renders a `<button role="switch">`, and a label does not name a button. Confirmed in the live DOM: tab stop 9 at 390px is `BUTTON[switch]` with an empty name, announced as "switch, on" with no indication of what it toggles.
- The wordmark link is nameless below 400px. TopBar.tsx:80 contains an `aria-hidden` SVG plus a text span carrying `max-[399px]:hidden`, so on a 390px phone the home link has nothing to announce. Confirmed as tab stop 2 with an empty name on both / and /night at 390px.
- Heading order on the home page starts at h3. The five filter groups render as h3 (Radix Accordion wraps its trigger in a heading), and they precede the page's only h1. Rendered DOM order: Media Type, Streaming Service, Score, Released, Genre, then "Your personal entertainment database." A screen-reader user scanning headings hits five filter names before the page title.
- Container borders are effectively invisible. `--border: #1f242c` measures 1.20:1 against the ground and 1.11:1 against panels, against a 3:1 requirement for meaningful non-text boundaries. `--border-strong` is 1.35:1. Card edges, input outlines and dividers carry real grouping information here and a low-vision user will not see them.
- Two of the three navigation landmarks are unnamed: the right-hand action nav at TopBar.tsx:110 and the footer nav at Footer.tsx:16. Separately, `--text-dim` on the accent fill measures 3.91:1, below the 4.5 floor. That combination only occurs on the "No art" poster placeholder (MediaCard.tsx:252, LibraryDeck.tsx:466), so it is narrow, but the type there is 10px.
- Nothing checks any of this automatically. ci.yml runs typecheck, test, lint and build; there is no axe dependency in package.json and no accessibility assertion in the test suite. Every item above is a regression that ships silently.

METHOD:
Read styles.css, TopBar.tsx, TopBarSearch.tsx, MediaCard.tsx, MediaDetail.tsx, FilterRail.tsx, accordion.tsx, __root.tsx and the route files; computed all 27 token contrast pairs from the hex values with the WCAG relative-luminance formula; rendered /, /about, /contact, /methodology, /privacy, /terms and /night in Chromium at 390px and 1440px and read heading order, landmarks, accessible names, input labelling and computed focus outlines out of the live DOM, plus 22 sequential tab stops per viewport. I could not check the database-backed pages (title, person, collections, calendar, play) in a browser because this box has no route to Supabase, so their heading order and control names are judged from source only. One early reading of a missing focus ring on the filter accordion turned out to be `transition-all` animating the outline width; after a 1.2s settle it measures the full 2px, so that is not a defect.

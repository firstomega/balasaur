SCORE_AGREE: yes

SUMMARY:
The shared pieces are real and the big pages use them. Home, collections, calendar and the title pages all go through MediaCard, MediaGrid, ScoreBadge and ScrollRail, and styles.css defines 95 design tokens with contrast ratios written into the comments. What is inconsistent is everything around those pieces. /about, /contact and /methodology each render the footer twice, because __root.tsx line 206 already renders it for every route and those three route files import and render it again; at 390px both copies are byte-identical and 226px tall on a 1670px page, so a quarter of the About page is footer and half of that is a repeat. Type and spacing have no scale: 24 distinct arbitrary pixel font sizes, 8 distinct corner radii against a single 5px radius token, and 16 distinct hard-coded max-widths for page containers. Colour discipline leaks in the same way. night.$code.tsx writes #9fe6a0 ten times when --rating is already exactly #9fe6a0, and MediaCard's watchlist gold #e8b84b is a near-miss of the --media-movie token #e8b552, so two golds two percent apart now coexist. styles.css opens by stating "All colors MUST use oklch format" and then defines 39 hex values against 5 oklch ones.

STRENGTHS:
- The CLAUDE.md no-duplicate rule holds where it matters most. ScoreBadge is imported by 7 files, MediaCard/MediaGrid cover the home and calendar grids, and there are only 4 raw overflow-x-auto blocks in 28k lines, so nobody rebuilt ScrollRail.
- The token layer is genuinely designed, not scaffolded. --text-dim carries a comment explaining it was lightened because 3.76 was under the 4.5 contrast floor, and --primary-foreground carries the same reasoning. That is a real accessibility decision recorded in the file.
- No horizontal overflow anywhere I rendered. Document scrollWidth stayed at 375 of 390 and 1425 of 1440 across /, /about, /contact, /methodology, /privacy, /terms and /night.
- TopBar and the page shell are consistent across every static route; the skip-to-content link in __root.tsx is present and correctly sr-only until focused.

GAPS:
- Duplicate footer on three of the five pages a reviewer actually reads. Confirmed rendered, not just in source: footer count is 2 on /about, /contact and /methodology at both 390px and 1440px, and the two nodes' text is identical. The TMDB and OMDb attribution, the legal nav and the copyright all print twice. On pages that are already short and mostly boilerplate, doubling the boilerplate is the wrong direction for an AdSense content review.
- The same bug class is one import away on /calendar and /play, which also render their own Footer. They currently show zero footers only because those routes need the database and fell over in this sandbox, so the duplication is latent rather than fixed.
- No type scale. text-[11px] appears 96 times, text-[10px] 67, text-[12px] 54, then a long tail of one-off sizes: 8, 16, 19, 21, 24, 29, 30, 34, 36, 64px each used exactly once. Every new heading picks a fresh number.
- No spacing or width scale either. Page containers use max-w-[1600px], [1240px], [1200px], [1160px], [1100px], [960px], [860px], [760px], [720px], [680px] and six more, mixed with Tailwind's named max-w-md/lg/sm/2xl. Reading width on /privacy is 720px, on /profile 760px, on /account 860px, for the same kind of prose page.
- The newest surface is the least consistent one. night.$code.tsx lines 1020 to 1080 hand-roll a horizontal result card with its own poster sizing, its own title and meta typography and its own chip styles, rather than adding a horizontal variant to MediaCard. It correctly reuses ScoreBadge, which shows the hand-rolling was a choice, not an oversight.
- Colours bypass their own tokens. Outside ProviderIcon, which legitimately needs brand hexes, there are 19 hard-coded hex values in TSX. Four of them account for 38 uses: #e8b84b, #9fe6a0, #c75d6e, #e08aa4. Two of those four already exist as tokens under different names, so a future palette change will move half the UI and leave the other half behind.

METHOD:
Read styles.css, __root.tsx, Footer.tsx, MediaCard.tsx and the Movie Night routes; grepped src for arbitrary font sizes, radii, max-widths and hex literals; ran a dev server and drove Chromium over the nine static routes at 390px and 1440px, counting footer and header nodes and checking scrollWidth. Could not check the database-driven pages (title, person, collections, calendar, play) in the browser because Supabase is unreachable from this sandbox, so their spacing and density at real widths is unverified.
